# Competitor Pattern Public Page Pass

Goal: make Ferocity's public pages reflect the strongest useful patterns from the platforms Chris referenced without cloning branding, making fake live claims, or adding clutter.

## Reference Patterns

### ScaleClients-style funnel

- Lead with the outcome: qualified leads, booked appointments, and revenue.
- Explain the system in a few simple steps.
- Use short application/audit flow before a call.
- Show that follow-up and show-up support matter after the lead arrives.
- Track the feedback loop from traffic to booked income.

### BabyLoveGrowth-style SEO/GEO autopilot

- Present SEO as a repeatable engine, not one-off blog writing.
- Include website/business analysis, search visibility, AI-search visibility, content, authority, and reporting.
- Keep quality and review control clear so it does not sound like spam.

### QuickFrame-style video ads

- Show that a rough idea or business asset can become a script, scenes, voiceover, and platform-ready brief.
- Be honest that rendered video and direct publishing require providers/credits/accounts.
- Keep videos short and ad-focused.

### Jobber / Housecall-style operating basics

- Make jobs, bids, estimates, invoices, receipts, materials, reminders, and worker day plans easy to understand.
- Do not bury the simple mode behind advanced AI language.
- Be honest that payment processing and external providers require setup.

## Page Changes To Make

- `/demo`: show the system as an end-to-end working loop: find demand, capture qualified lead, follow up, do the work, get paid, reuse proof, improve.
- `/features`: group features by outcomes instead of only modules, and include SEO/GEO, video ad brief, field-service basics, and command center.
- `/pricing`: make each tier feel useful and distinct; explain Growth as an engine, not "one report."
- `/start`: make onboarding feel like "choose what you want Ferocity to handle first," with skip-friendly wording.

## Guardrails

- No fake claims that live ads, public publishing, rendered video, SMS, or payment collection are active without keys/providers.
- No copied brand language or direct cloning.
- No long repetitive paragraphs.
- Keep calls to action obvious: free grader, demo, pricing, start setup.

## Verification

- Run lint, typecheck, public guard, and build after changes.
