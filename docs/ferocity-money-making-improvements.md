# Ferocity Money-Making Improvement Pass

This pass focuses on making Ferocity easier to buy, easier to start, and easier to trust.

## Product Priorities

1. One obvious start path: grade the business, pick what should be handled first, and let Ferocity build the setup plan.
2. First-week value: show the customer what Ferocity can improve right away.
3. AI Workforce packaging: make digital employees easy to understand without hiding the direct tools.
4. Business Grader conversion: every audit should point to the plan or workflow that fixes the gap.
5. Managed Operator: sell outcomes for owners who do not want to manage software.
6. Proof-based growth: completed work should become reviews, testimonials, SEO, posts, and ads.
7. Simple money tracking: jobs, bids, materials, receipts, worker payments, invoice balances, and profit should stay visible.
8. Owner daily brief: routine items should batch; urgent money, risk, customer, and approval items should rise up.
9. Tier clarity: locked/provider-gated tools should explain what they unlock without pretending they are live.
10. Keep the product from becoming more work: every major page should answer “what do I do next?”

## Launch Standard

Ferocity should feel like:

> Pick the work you want off your plate. Ferocity sets up the system, prepares the work, watches the queue, and brings you the decisions that matter.

It should not feel like:

> Learn 40 software modules before anything useful happens.

## Do Not Overpromise

Ferocity can prepare, route, draft, recommend, queue, log, and automate approved work.

Live customer sends, public publishing, ad spend, phone calls, managed payouts, and provider actions must remain tied to provider readiness, customer permissions, approval rules, and usage controls.
