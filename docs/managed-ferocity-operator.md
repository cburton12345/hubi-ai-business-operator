# Managed Ferocity Operator

Goal: offer customers a tier where Ferocity is managed for them, while also making it possible for Chris to run many customers with minimal daily involvement.

## Product Promise

Managed Ferocity is for owners who want the outcome but do not want to learn every screen.

Plain-English promise:

> We help run Ferocity for you. The AI watches the business, prepares the work, and brings only important decisions to you.

## What Managed Includes

- Initial business audit and setup plan.
- Business Info / brand memory setup.
- Website form or hosted page setup path.
- Lead-source tracking setup.
- Follow-up and review workflow setup.
- SEO/GEO and content calendar setup.
- Campaign/funnel package setup.
- Weekly or monthly operating review.
- AI action queue review.
- Owner-only escalation for money, risk, legal/safety, angry customers, failed automations, or low-confidence work.

## What AI Should Handle First

- Find missing setup pieces.
- Watch leads, estimates, invoices, jobs, reviews, receipts, reminders, and marketing gaps.
- Prepare drafts and next steps.
- Route simple work to the right Ferocity module.
- Log every action.
- Escalate only when the rules say a human is needed.

## What Chris Should See

Chris should not need to open every customer workspace every day.

Ferocity should show:

- Customers needing owner/operator attention.
- High-value leads or revenue risk.
- Failed provider connections.
- AI actions blocked by missing info.
- Content/campaigns ready for approval.
- Billing/payment setup problems.
- Customers that need a human check-in.

## Escalation Rules

Immediate human review when:

- Customer complaint or negative review.
- Large unpaid invoice or failed payment.
- High-value lead with no response.
- Legal, safety, or compliance issue.
- Ad spend, public publishing, payment request, or customer-facing send needs approval.
- Provider failure or missing key blocks promised work.
- AI confidence is low or business facts are missing.

## Guardrails

- Managed does not mean Ferocity secretly spends money, publishes publicly, moves customer money, or sends customer messages without permission.
- Managed payments, managed marketing, and rendered media need written terms and clear fees.
- Use existing Ferocity workflows, records, approvals, and logs. Do not create duplicate CRM, marketing, or payment systems.

## Implementation Notes

- Public pricing should show Managed Operator as a custom tier/add-on.
- Start page should include managed setup as a requested plan.
- AI Workforce should describe Managed Mode as the operating model.
- Billing should surface managed services and manual terms clearly.
- Automation Command remains the control center for what can run, what needs review, and what is blocked.
