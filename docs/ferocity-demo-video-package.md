# Ferocity Demo Video Package

## Purpose

Create a short promo/walkthrough clip that makes Ferocity easy to understand for a first-time business owner.

The video should sell this simple idea:

Ferocity watches the business, finds what needs attention, prepares the next action, and helps the owner get more booked income without losing control.

## Primary Audience

Service business owners who are busy, overwhelmed, and tired of losing money because follow-up, reviews, invoices, jobs, or marketing slipped.

Examples:

- Roofing
- HVAC
- Cleaning
- Remodeling
- Landscaping
- Local service companies
- Owner-led businesses with leads, jobs, invoices, and reviews

## Core Promise

Stop losing jobs because follow-up, reviews, invoices, or daily tasks slipped.

Ferocity helps service businesses respond to leads, follow up on estimates, organize work, collect money, request reviews, and know what needs attention today.

AI prepares the work. The owner stays in control.

## Video Format

- Length: 15-45 seconds preferred, with a 60-second version only when the channel needs more context
- Style: Product walkthrough + owner pain story
- Tone: serious, practical, confident, not hype-heavy
- Format: 16:9 primary, with 9:16 cutdown later
- CTA: Run the free business grader

## Script

### 0-8 Seconds: The Problem

Visual:
Fast cuts of a busy owner checking phone, email, invoices, job notes, and missed calls.

Voiceover:
"Most businesses do not lose money because the owner does not care. They lose money because too many things slip."

On-screen text:
Missed leads. Forgotten follow-up. Unpaid invoices. No reviews. No clear next step.

### 8-18 Seconds: Introduce Ferocity

Visual:
Ferocity dashboard opens. Owner command center shows hot leads, pipeline, review asks, and money alerts.

Voiceover:
"Ferocity gives your business an AI operating system that watches the work, finds the gaps, and tells you what needs attention."

On-screen text:
Ferocity watches the business and finds the next action.

### 18-32 Seconds: Lead And Follow-Up

Visual:
A new lead appears. Ferocity flags it as urgent. A suggested reply is prepared. Owner approves.

Voiceover:
"When a lead comes in, Ferocity helps you respond fast. When an estimate sits too long, it prepares the follow-up. When a customer needs a callback, it keeps that work visible."

On-screen text:
New lead -> reply draft -> owner approval -> follow-up scheduled

### 32-46 Seconds: Jobs, Money, And Reviews

Visual:
Job board, invoice reminder, review request, before/after proof.

Voiceover:
"Ferocity helps organize jobs, track payments, remind you about invoices, request reviews, and turn completed work into proof for the next customer."

On-screen text:
Jobs. Invoices. Reviews. Proof. Revenue.

### 46-55 Seconds: Growth Loop

Visual:
Lead source attribution, SEO/review/Google profile drafts, campaign ideas.

Voiceover:
"It connects the work back to revenue, so you can see which leads, pages, reviews, and campaigns are creating real booked income."

On-screen text:
Know what creates booked income.

### 55-65 Seconds: Owner Control

Visual:
Approval screen with buttons: approve, edit, pause, keep manual.

Voiceover:
"AI prepares the work. You choose what it can run, what needs approval, and what stays manual."

On-screen text:
Autopilot with owner control.

### 65-75 Seconds: CTA

Visual:
Business Grader page. Score/report preview. CTA button.

Voiceover:
"Start with the free business grader. See what is leaking money, then let Ferocity help you build the system to fix it."

On-screen text:
Run the free business grader.

CTA:
Grade My Business Free
