# Ferocity Launch Video Creative

## The Truth We Are Selling

Businesses did not really want another CRM, website builder, isolated AI agent, or library of workflows they still have to remember to run. They wanted the work to keep moving.

Ferocity gives human employees and AI employees one shared Business Brain. It remembers what happened, watches for meaningful change, determines what should happen next, performs or prepares authorized work, verifies the result, and continues watching.

The emotional outcome is relief. The product behavior is coordinated persistence.

## Primary Hook

**Your business should not wait for someone to remember.**

Supporting line:

**Ferocity keeps the work moving.**

## Claim Boundaries

- Say Ferocity prepares or performs **authorized** work.
- Say it connects and coordinates the business; do not imply every provider works without setup.
- Show human judgment remaining in the loop for real decisions.
- Do not claim guaranteed revenue, zero missed work, or fully autonomous operation in every configuration.
- Do not use fake product screenshots or generated unreadable UI as evidence.

## 12-Second Sora Launch Hook

The generated clip is an emotional opening, not the whole product demonstration. It should be followed by real Ferocity screens in the finished 25-35 second ad.

### Visual arc

1. A capable service-business team is overwhelmed by fragmented work.
2. The business becomes coordinated; office, field, customer, schedule, and money move as one operation.
3. The owner leaves with confidence while the authorized operating loop continues.

### Voiceover

“Your business shouldn’t wait for someone to remember. Ferocity keeps the work moving.”

## Recommended Full Ad Structure

- 0-4 seconds: emotional problem and hook.
- 4-12 seconds: generated transformation clip.
- 12-24 seconds: real Ferocity screens showing Notice, Decide, Act, Verify, Continue.
- 24-30 seconds: authority controls and a real human decision.
- 30-35 seconds: “Most software helps you run the business. Ferocity helps run it with you.” plus Start Ferocity and View Plans.

Use exact text and branding in post-production rather than asking a video model to render interface copy.
