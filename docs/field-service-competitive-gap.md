# Field-Service Competitive Gap

Ferocity should not try to be a Jobber or Housecall Pro clone. Those products are mature field-service systems. Ferocity should be credible on the basics, then win through AI command, growth, owner visibility, and cross-business automation.

## What Jobber / Housecall Pro Do Better Today

- Polished mobile field app.
- Mature quote-to-job-to-invoice flow.
- Mature scheduling and dispatch.
- Mature customer portal/client hub.
- Production payment workflows.
- Accounting sync, especially QuickBooks-style workflows.
- Field checklists, photo reports, job forms, and repeatable trade templates.
- Easier onboarding for a classic service business.

## Ferocity Must Be Credible Here

- Add lead, customer, estimate, job, invoice, payment, receipt, worker, material, and reminder manually.
- Keep the daily job flow obvious: capture, bid, schedule, dispatch, materials, invoice, collect, portal, proof, review, accounting.
- Show payment readiness without implying managed payouts are live.
- Keep customer portal links easy to find.
- Keep accounting exports/provider sync clearly gated.
- Keep mobile pages simple enough for a field worker to use.

## Ferocity's Differentiator

- AI watches the business and tells the owner what matters.
- AI prepares follow-up, reviews, reminders, content, proof reuse, and owner summaries.
- Marketing, SEO, reviews, leads, jobs, invoices, and revenue live in the same loop.
- Connected systems can feed the owner command center.
- Multi-business owners can see money, risk, and opportunities without opening every platform.

## Near-Term Product Priority

1. Keep Service Command as the plain daily work loop.
2. Improve customer portal usefulness without pretending it is a full payment portal until providers are ready.
3. Add provider-gated QuickBooks/accounting readiness.
4. Keep manual forms fast and visible.
5. Improve mobile field layouts before broad contractor launch.
6. Build Stripe Connect only after fee policy, terms, onboarding, webhooks, payouts, disputes, refunds, and account status checks are ready.
