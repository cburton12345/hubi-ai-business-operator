# Operations & Workforce Management

Ferocity Operations & Workforce Management is the field-operations layer for owners, office managers, crew leaders, employees, and subcontractors.

It is intentionally separate from CRM, marketing, estimates, invoices, and AI Walkthrough while linking to those systems through shared tenant, customer, job, assignment, worker, and event IDs.

## Current MVP

- Workers and subcontractors
- Dispatch assignments
- Daily schedule visibility
- Clock in and clock out records
- Payroll-ready time entries
- Receipt and expense ledger
- Mileage ledger
- Material logs
- Job-cost snapshot from tracked costs
- AI dispatcher recommendations
- Role-based experience map
- Schedule-board views for today, week, crew, and job
- Optional location pings with consent/verification metadata
- Field proof records for photos, videos, receipts, documents, and AI Walkthrough output
- Customer update drafts for arrival, delay, completion, and proof messages
- Payroll export drafts for CSV, QuickBooks, Gusto, ADP, and manual review
- Provider readiness records for payroll, accounting, calendar, GPS, SMS, email, and storage

## Product Boundaries

Ferocity does not become a payroll processor, accounting system, GPS surveillance product, or customer messaging carrier.

Payroll, accounting, SMS, email, GPS, and calendar providers stay modular. Ferocity prepares clean records, review queues, exports, and provider-ready integration points.

## Future Provider Integrations

- Live QuickBooks, Gusto, ADP, and accounting sends
- Calendar sync and visual dispatch drag-and-drop
- Continuous GPS background tracking
- Receipt file storage plus AI extraction from uploaded images
- Customer arrival/completion sends through SMS/email providers
- Owner Command Center escalation events from late crews, payroll risk, cost overruns, and customer issues
